# Code of conduct

## The standard

This is a research repository. The subject is contested, the corpus states positions it
cannot prove and labels them as such, and disagreement is the point of publishing.

**Argue with the work, not the person.** A sharply argued refutation of a paper is
welcome and is the highest-value contribution available. An attack on the author of the
paper is not.

## Expected

- Criticise claims, evidence, reasoning and wording as hard as you like
- Attach sources. A contested `[E]` claim is settled by a primary source, not by volume
- Accept a correction without defensiveness, including when you wrote the thing corrected
- Assume the other person read carefully and reached a different conclusion

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, insults, demeaning comments
- Harassment, whether public or in private messages
- Publishing another person's private information
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or reopening a settled decision without new argument
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any space where
somebody represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report to `conduct@myneogroup.com`. Reports are handled privately and the reporter's
identity is not disclosed to the reported person without consent.

| Severity | Response |
|---|---|
| First, minor | Private correction, with an explanation of what was wrong |
| Repeated, or a single serious incident | Temporary ban from interaction, stated duration |
| Sustained, or harassment | Permanent ban |

A maintainer who breaches this document is subject to it on the same terms. The
maintainer group is small and that clause exists because it needs to be written down
rather than assumed.

## Attribution
