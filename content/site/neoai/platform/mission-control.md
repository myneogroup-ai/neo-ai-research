# Mission Control

## Header

**One Mission state. Three ways to see it.**

Mission Control is the operating-system view of NEO AI. Not a chat window with a progress bar.

## Body

A chat interface shows you an answer. Mission Control shows you the work. FOCUS, NORMAL and JARVIS are three projections of one Mission state, not three applications. Changing mode changes what the person sees, never what NEO is doing: execution is not restarted, no second Mission is created and the backend truth does not change. Execution location is equally independent of projection; the Mission belongs to NEO, not to the device that started it. `[D] (mission.state, S2)` `[D] (mission-control, S3)`

| Projection | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **FOCUS** | The conversation, the recommendation and the decision. As immediately understandable as a consumer assistant |
| **NORMAL** | Adds the execution plan, the active agents, artefact progress and evidence |
| **JARVIS** | Adds the command core, live orchestration, the observed build and the task graph |

What is constant in all three: navigation, Missions, the needs-you queue, the composer with Intelligence Profile and approval policy, the Human Gate card, and a universal right inspector with **Progress**, **Output** and **Context**. Approval is a property of the Mission, not of the view.

## What it is designed to show

- The **live task graph**, with the state of each node
- **Agents coordinated by LILI**, and what each is doing
- **Real model activity**, recorded as execution provenance of a completed unit of work
- **Evidence flow**, and the chain from a claim back to its origins
- **Operational decision events**: a contradiction detected, verification activated, evidence coverage below policy, a model replaced, a source rejected, a tool failed, a human approval required
- **Human approvals**, and whether one is still valid
- **Mission Replay**, to watch a completed Mission again, and **Challenge This Conclusion**, to force a conclusion back through verification

## Product truth: nothing is simulated

No fake agents, progress, tests, evidence, cost or verified state. A capability not yet connected is shown as such, and the interface never implies a result that was not produced. NEO responds with visible progressive work, rationale summaries, evidence, decisions and provenance, and **never private chain-of-thought**: if the explanatory content of an audit trail is generated text, the audit trail can be confabulated, and a record that can be confabulated by the component it records is not a control. `[D] (mission-control, S3)`

Product design references and screenshots published in NEO AI material are labelled as prototype interface with synthetic fixture data; none of their numbers is a measured result, a benchmark or a commercial commitment.

## Status

One Mission state across projections and devices: in operation. `[D] (mission.state, S2)` The three projections and the right inspector: implemented and in validation. `[D] (mission-control, S3)`
