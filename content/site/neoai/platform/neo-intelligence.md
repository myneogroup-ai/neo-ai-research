# NEO Intelligence

## Header

**Not a dashboard. Not a database. An intelligence layer.**

## Body

NEO Intelligence is designed as the orchestration and reasoning layer above intelligence providers: open-source intelligence, corporate registries and beneficial ownership, blockchain and wallet intelligence, AML and sanctions screening through licensed providers, adverse media, cyber threat, documents, relationships, timelines and evidence, resolved into one provenance-tracked, evidence-backed report under a human approval gate. `[D] (neo-intelligence, S4)`

The path is fixed: source, agent, evidence, finding, Human Gate, report. Confidence is never presented as certainty.

It is designed for banks and regulated institutions (KYC and KYB, enhanced due diligence, transaction context), law firms (litigation support, asset tracing, evidence assembly) and corporates and funds (counterparty, M&A and investment due diligence).

## Safeguards, locked

Lawful access only, under applicable law and contract. The connector roadmap is subject to provider access and licensing. NEO does not own or resell third-party databases. High-impact conclusions require human review. NEO orchestrates screening results and is not an adjudication authority: no law-enforcement powers, no final legal or sanctions determinations. Edge and customer-private compute can preprocess large evidence sets before cross-source reasoning; local compute changes where processing happens, not the person's legal rights to data.

## Status

Designed and specified. Not launched. Counted at zero revenue in every scenario. `[D] (neo-intelligence, S4)`
