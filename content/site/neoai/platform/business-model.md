# Business model

## Header

**Several layers, two potential businesses**

## Body

| Layer | What it is |
|---|---|
| 1 NEO SaaS | Subscriptions for individuals, professionals and companies, B2C and B2B on the same core |
| 2 Vertical intelligence products | Dedicated environments for areas such as research, legal, finance, software development, NEO Intelligence and enterprise operations |
| 3 Usage and compute | Subscriptions include a level of NEO Credits, with additional intelligence and compute capacity when required |
| 4 Enterprise deployments | Private infrastructure, proprietary data, private models, advanced security, dedicated environments and custom integrations |
| 5 NEO infrastructure technology | Potential licensing of the orchestration, routing and compute-efficiency stack to the AI industry. Optionality, counted at zero |

Layers 1 to 4 form the first business, an Intelligence Operating System used by customers. Layer 5 is a potential second business, an intelligence and compute optimisation layer used by the AI industry itself. `[D] (infrastructure-licensing, S5)`

Because the customer unit, NEO Credits, is decoupled from provider tokens, every gain in intelligence per compute can lower NEO's cost to serve without changing what the customer buys. That is the design intent; it is not a measured margin. `[D] (neo-credits, S4)`

This page carries no price, no revenue figure and no financial projection. Nothing on the public pages of this site is an offer of securities.
