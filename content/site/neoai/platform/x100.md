# NEO x100

## Header

**More useful intelligence per dollar, per GPU, per token and per watt**

Not 100 times more parameters. Not 100 times more GPUs. Not brute-force intelligence.

## Body

NEO x100 is the long-term R&D objective of an order-of-magnitude improvement in **verified intelligence per unit of compute**, through orchestration, specialisation, parallelism, verification, memory, reuse, edge execution, hardware-aware routing and adaptive resource allocation. `[D] (x100, S5)`

**x100 is an R&D objective, not a measured result.** No benchmark performance, compute saving or multiple is stated or guaranteed on this site.

## The eight optimisation layers

| Layer | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Mixture-of-Agents | Independent specialised agents research, reason, build, critique and verify parts of one Mission; NEO synthesises their outputs | `[D] (x100.mixture-of-agents, S3)` |
| 02 Adaptive Model Cascades | Start on the least expensive capable model; escalate only when confidence, complexity, verification or policy requires | `[D] (x100.adaptive-cascades, S3)` |
| 03 Dynamic Capability Routing | Choose what performs each unit of work and where it executes, by capability, never by brand | `[D] (core.routing, S2)` |
| 04 Semantic Compute Reuse | Reuse verified, still-valid, authorised intelligence instead of regenerating it | `[D] (x100.semantic-reuse, S4)` |
| 05 Speculative Parallel Intelligence | Explore candidate approaches in parallel, terminate weak branches early, concentrate compute on promising or disputed paths | `[D] (x100.speculative-parallel, S4)` |
| 06 Verification-Driven Compute Allocation | Cheaper models first, independent verifiers decide where more reasoning is necessary | `[D] (x100.verification-driven-allocation, S4)` |
| 07 Hybrid API, Private and Self-Hosted Execution | Route workloads across edge, frontier APIs, private models, specialist systems and NEO-controlled or self-hosted open models | `[D] (x100.hybrid-execution, S4)` |
| 08 Memory and Context Optimisation | Build the minimum authoritative context each invocation needs | `[D] (x100.context-optimisation, S3)` |

## The cheapest model does not automatically win

x100 optimisation is constrained by quality. NEO minimises cost, latency, data movement and unnecessary compute **subject to** the Mission's required quality, verification, security, jurisdiction, authority, privacy and availability envelope. The best execution path is the least expensive permitted one that satisfies that envelope, never a cheaper or more local path that degrades the result.

Three Missions, three paths. A simple extraction runs on a low-cost capable model, passes verification and is done, and frontier compute is never touched. Strategic reasoning escalates on confidence and policy, and only the hard part reaches a frontier model before independent verification. In a regulated jurisdiction, routing is constrained by jurisdiction, security policy and authority before cost is considered, and the compliant path wins even if it is not the cheapest.

## Compute before tokens

Not every task needs a frontier model, and not every task needs the cloud. Before a workload becomes tokens, NEO determines whether a deterministic tool, a local runtime, a database query, a parser, a compiler, a vision system or a specialist model can do it more accurately and economically. Four rules: reuse before recompute; tools before tokens; minimum data movement; stop when done.

NEO does not pay frontier-model economics to discover which information deserves frontier-model intelligence.

## Mission economics, not per-call price

The unit of optimisation is the cost per verified Mission outcome. A cheap first call that causes five retries costs more than one stronger call, and a premium model used strategically can lower total Mission cost. Compute can also increase when value justifies it: high-stakes Missions may deliberately spend more on independent analysis, deeper verification and red-team reasoning.

x100 measures net system efficiency in the denominator, including external inference, NEO-controlled, customer-private and user-owned compute, tools, orchestration, verification, retries, memory, data movement, human review and the cost of NEO itself.

## Status

Dynamic Capability Routing is in operation. `[D] (core.routing, S2)` Layers 01, 02 and 08 are implemented and in validation. Layers 04 to 07 are designed and specified. The x100 objective itself is unmeasured. `[D] (x100, S5)`
