# Deployment boundaries

## Header

**One system, several boundaries**

## Body

Private AI is an operating model rather than a hosting choice. The question an organisation is really asking is not where the servers are; it is which data crosses which boundary, under whose control, with what record.

NEO AI is designed as one system deployable against three boundaries: managed cloud, private cloud, and on-premises. The same governance applies at each, and the boundary is a property of the deployment rather than a different product.
