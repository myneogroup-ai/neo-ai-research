# Verticals and markets

## Header

**Multiple products, one core. A new vertical is not a new company.**

## Body

NEO AI is one product. Every vertical is the same Intelligence Operating System configured for an industry: its own sources, connectors, workflows, verifiers, evidence policy and compliance, over shared orchestration, identity, billing, models, agents, memory, governance, verification, compute and the Execution Fabric. No vertical builds its own execution stack, and incremental work concentrates in domain configuration, data, connectors, compliance and go-to-market. `[D] (core, S2)`

| Group | Verticals | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot-anchored today | Hospitality and Nightlife, Micro-Credit, Trading and Treasury | `[D] (verticals.pilot-anchored, S2)` |
| Built on the same core when demand is proven | Software Factory, Company Builder, Fintech Studio, Payments, Sport and Wellness, RWA Studio | `[D] (verticals.shared-core, S4)` |
| Counted at zero in every projection | Health and Care, Legal Ops, Travel | `[D] (verticals.zero-counted, S5)` |
| Premium roadmap vertical, counted at zero | NEO Intelligence | `[D] (neo-intelligence, S4)` |

Pilot counts and client identities are never disclosed.

Shared orchestration does not make two domains interchangeable. Domain validity is earned separately in each vertical: a verifier set matched to the domain's failure modes, an evidence policy, a licence position for each data source, an approval topology matched to who is professionally accountable, and evaluation against tasks drawn from the domain.

## Every customer class, one core

NEO AI is not an enterprise-only platform. The same core serves individuals and professionals (creators, developers, researchers, investors), organisations from SMEs to regulated institutions and large groups, and, where applicable and subject to the relevant product and legal framework, government and sovereign programmes. What differs by class is the capability envelope: permissions, data access, connectors, compliance, compute envelope, pricing, deployment, authority, security and tools. `[D] (market.b2g, S5)`

A private user can use legal or finance capabilities for research and analysis; an enterprise environment adds private data, enterprise connectors, governance and dedicated infrastructure. A consumer never gains access to restricted enterprise, regulated or licensed datasets because the same vertical exists.

## Status

The three pilot-anchored verticals are exercised in pilot programmes with external users. `[D] (verticals.pilot-anchored, S2)` General public availability, global, for individuals, professionals and organisations is targeted for early 2027. `[D] (availability.general, S5)`
