# Model sovereignty roadmap

## Header

**From API-dependent to hybrid intelligence infrastructure**

## Body

NEO does not own a frontier foundation model today and does not claim to. The staged strategy reduces structural dependence on any single provider while preserving access to whichever external frontier systems are strongest. The objective is not ideological independence. It is optimal intelligence, resilience and economics.

| Phase | What it means | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 API-first | Multi-provider orchestration of leading external systems through APIs; no provider is structurally mandatory. Selected local workloads follow Edge Compute, which is on the roadmap | `[D] (model-sovereignty.api-first, S2)` |
| 2 Hybrid | Frontier APIs continue while selected open-weight and private models are progressively deployed on NEO-controlled, private and self-hosted infrastructure; no third-party model IP is owned | `[D] (model-sovereignty.hybrid, S4)` |
| 3 NEO-optimised layer | A rising share of suitable workloads handled through fine-tuned, distilled, domain-specialised and task-specific models, reusable intelligence and evaluation-driven model selection | `[D] (model-sovereignty.neo-optimised, S5)` |

External frontier APIs remain available in every phase. Models remain replaceable. Compute providers and hardware backends remain replaceable. The system compounds.

Any model NEO deploys or optimises sits in the same worker layer as any external model and is addressed through the same adapter contract. The orchestration layer itself contains no weights.
