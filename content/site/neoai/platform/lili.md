# LILI

## Header

**LILI. The face of NEO AI.**

You talk to LILI. LILI mobilises the platform.

## Body

LILI is the conversational presence of NEO AI. A private user, a professional or an enterprise team opens NEO the way they would open any assistant, states an objective in their own language, and receives a result. LILI asks for the clarifications the objective is missing, shows what is being proposed, and keeps the work visible. `[D] (lili, S2)` Sources counted, claims verified with the evidence one click away, and one decision card whenever a Human Gate is required are implemented and in validation. `[D] (core.verification, S3)` `[D] (human-gates, S3)`

LILI is the face, NEO AI is the platform, Mission Control is the operating-system view. LILI is a luminous presence, never a human face, an avatar or a robot.

LILI is in operation inside My NEO Group and with external pilot users. `[D] (lili, S2)`

## A digital workforce behind one conversation

Where a traditional assistant gives one answer, LILI organises an entire digital workforce around an objective: research, legal, build, evidence, security, financial, risk-critic, verification and specialist agents, all coordinated through the same Intelligence Operating System. `[D] (core, S2)` `[D] (x100.mixture-of-agents, S3)`

An agent identity represents a **capability**, not a model and not a vendor. No agent is named after, defined by or bound to a foundation model. Which model served a unit of work is recorded as provenance of that work, which is what makes an executor replaceable without the user being told a different story about who did the work. The agents are not personalities: no invented biography, no claimed preference, no simulated emotion.

## Coordinating is not a privilege

LILI proposes Missions and never grants the authority to run them. Every act is recorded, attributable to a named principal, evaluated against policy and bounded by a permission envelope resolved before the act. LILI's reach never exceeds the authority of the person whose session it is acting in, and mandatory Human Gates are never bypassed. `[D] (lili, S2)` `[D] (human-gates, S3)`

## LILI Always-on (roadmap)

**You do not open LILI. LILI is already there.**

LILI Always-on is designed as two things together. Continuous presence: LILI stays active in the background on the devices the person authorises, follows running Missions to completion, watches the signals entrusted to it (a KPI, an inbox, a deadline, a build) and alerts the person the moment a decision is theirs. Hands-free voice: a wake word such as "Hey LILI" and a continuous spoken conversation, on the phone, the computer and other authorised devices, with the same Mission whether the person speaks, types or switches device. `[D] (lili.always-on, S4)`

Four rules are fixed. Always-on never bypasses a mandatory approval. The person switches it on, pauses it or turns it off, per device, at any time. Listening is not training: presence is a permission, never training consent. FOCUS, NORMAL and JARVIS show the same work.

## Status

LILI, the conversational interface: in operation in controlled deployment. `[D] (lili, S2)` LILI Always-on: designed and specified, on the roadmap. `[D] (lili.always-on, S4)`
