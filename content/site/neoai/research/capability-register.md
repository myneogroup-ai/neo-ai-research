# Capability Register

## Header

**What exists, what is specified, and what is planned**

## Body

This ledger is the answer to a question the rest of the site does not get to avoid: how much of what is described actually runs.

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Operational** | Running and publicly available |
| **In pilot** | Running in a controlled or limited deployment. Counts and client identities are never disclosed |
| **Validating** | Implemented, undergoing validation |
| **Specified** | Designed and specified. Not running |
| **Planned** | Planned or exploratory |

## State of the platform

NEO AI and LILI are in operational deployment inside My NEO Group and in controlled pilot programmes with external users. General public availability, global, is targeted for early 2027. Register version 2.0 records this as a Founder determination of 2026-10-18 and restructures the register to the NEO x100 Intelligence Operating System architecture; retired keys resolve to named successors and are never reissued. Version 2.0 was ratified by the Founder on 2026-10-18; version 1.3 had been ratified on 2026-08-13.

The ledger is machine-readable at `/capabilities.json` and every research publication resolves its capability references against it at build time, so a paper and this page cannot disagree.

**No status in this ledger has been verified by an independent third party.** The Program has no external certification and no completed third-party assessment published against the platform. That is stated here rather than left to be discovered.
