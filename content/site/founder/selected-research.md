# Selected research

## Header

**Selected research**

The NEO AI Research Program publishes its corpus at [neoai.myneogroup.com/research](https://neoai.myneogroup.com/research/). This page is my selection from it, with why each one matters and where I would push back on it.

Each entry carries the paper's abstract, unaltered, and links to the canonical text. I do not reproduce the papers here.

## The six in Public Wave 1

`NEO-AI-P-001` Introducing NEO AI: Intelligence Orchestrated. The position statement, and the one that discloses how much of the platform exists.

`NEO-AI-P-007` A Note on Responsible AI Capability Claims. The standard the rest is written under, and the one I would read first if I were assessing us.

**From Model Intelligence to System Intelligence.** The centre of the argument. Identifier requested and not yet allocated, so cite it by title.

`NEO-AI-TN-001` Bounding the Autonomy Budget. Short, and the most directly useful to anyone building.

`NEO-AI-TN-002` Detecting Circular Corroboration in an Evidence Chain. Five documents restating one press release are one source.

`NEO-AI-TN-003` What Belongs in a Mission Event. What a governance record has to answer, and what it must never contain.
