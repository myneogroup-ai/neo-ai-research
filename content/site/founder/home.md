# Home

**Mickael Mosse**

I am building NEO AI, an Intelligence Operating System for people and organisations that have to justify their decisions. It is in operation today inside My NEO Group and with external pilot users, and general public availability is targeted for early 2027.

The thesis is short. Model capability is becoming abundant and orchestration is not. The durable asset is not the model an organisation chose; it is the layer that decides which model runs, on what data, under whose authority, against what evidence, and with what record left behind. That layer is what survives the next release, and almost nobody is building it properly.
