Organisation

NEO AI Research Program

The research function of NEO TECH LABS. It publishes architectural argument, a capability register and a controlled vocabulary, and it publishes corrections rather than quietly fixing them.

What it publishes

Research papers, institutional publications and technical notes. Every material statement carries a claim class and every capability reference resolves to the Capability Register. The standards that govern all of it are on the methodology page.

What it does not claim

The Program publishes no benchmark, holds no reproducible comparison against any other system and claims none. No page asserts that NEO AI is more capable than any named model or product.

Structure

The Program is part of NEO TECH LABS TECHNOLOGY L.L.C, Iris Bay, Business Bay, Dubai, UAE, a subsidiary of My NEO Group. Research is written by Mickael Mosse, whose founder thesis and annotation are published separately at mickaelmosse.ai.

A single author writes, reviews and approves this corpus today. That concentration is a stated risk of the programme, not an oversight, and named external reviewers are being recruited to separate authorship from approval.

Corrections

Corrections are published on the updates page and carried in a separate Atom feed, because a reader who has already cited a document needs corrections to it more than they need new documents.
