## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-18

Alignment with the NEO AI x100 Intelligence Operating System architecture authority and
with the current state of the platform. Capability register v2.0 ratified by the Founder
on 2026-10-18, including the thirteen S3 rows proposed by inference from the operational MVP.

### Changed
- **Platform state.** NEO AI and LILI are in operational deployment inside My NEO Group
  and in controlled pilot programmes with external users. General public availability,
  global, is targeted for early 2027. Every text that described the platform as a
  specification that was not running is corrected, as a published correction with a
  version-history row, not silently.
- **Capability register v2.0.** Restructured to the x100 architecture: 51 capabilities,
  15 retired keys with named successors. The orchestration keys move to `core.*`;
  `foundation-model` is replaced by the three-phase model sovereignty roadmap; deployment
  keys follow the five deployment patterns. New entries for LILI Always-on, the three
  projections, Observe Build, Intelligence Profiles, Human Gates, the eight x100 layers,
  the Execution Fabric, NEO Edge Compute, verticals, markets, NEO Credits, the learning
  loop and infrastructure licensing. Each row records the basis of its status.
- **Controlled vocabulary v2.0.** 27 x100 terms added; Validation Layer renamed
  Verification Layer; five retired or refused terms added to the prohibited synonyms
  with the term to use instead.
- **Publications revised**: P-001 v1.1, P-007 v1.1, R-009 v1.1, TN-001 v1.1, TN-002
  v1.1, TN-003 v1.1, and the held Wave 1b papers R-001, R-003 and R-005. Front-matter
  claim counts regenerated by script; canonical URLs corrected from placeholders.
- **Site content** for neoai.myneogroup.com rewritten to the x100 structure. New pages:
  x100, Execution Fabric and Edge Compute, Intelligence Profiles and NEO Credits, model
  sovereignty, verticals and markets, NEO Intelligence, business model.
- Founder site pages updated for the operational state.
- `schema/entities.jsonld`: operator named as NEO TECH LABS TECHNOLOGY L.L.C; product
  description and availability note updated; register dataset at version 2.0.

### Added
- Conformance checks CAP-02 (a status token in any public text must equal the register)
  and CAP-03 (retired capability keys are refused). This closes the status-token half of
  the verb-to-status gap recorded in P-007; the verb lint itself is still unbuilt.
- `scripts/count_claims.py`, the only permitted source of front-matter claim counts.
- Generated capability register now renders the platform state, every row's note and
  the retired-key table; the glossary index renders every prohibited synonym.

### Moved
- The August 2026 research HTML delta moved from `production/` to `archive/superseded/`.
  It renders statuses and keys that register v2.0 supersedes and must not be deployed.

### Open before release
- A new research HTML delta built from corpus 0.2.0.
